Listen to me speak about my life and career on the Igniting Change podcast, season 8, episode 3: "Why Maps Matter: Turning Community Data Into Resilience Tools."

I discuss my lifelong love of maps, how I got started working in open data, being on the MapSwipe Governance Team, and how my background in participatory art connects with my present day work.

This podcast was a lot of fun to do, and I'm excited to share it with all of you!
